About ITAREVO

Travel is complex enough. The tools should feel simple.

ITAREVO is a travel technology company building a calmer way to plan, organize and manage journeys.

What we are building

ITAREVO Travel is an intelligent all-in-one travel companion. It brings the practical parts of a journey together: trip plans, itineraries, travel information, preparation, documents, money tracking and useful tools for the days you are away.

The product is designed for travellers who want structure without turning a trip into an administrative project. It keeps the important context close, then gets out of the way when the journey begins.

A product with honest boundaries

ITAREVO is currently an iOS-first travel organizer and companion. It supports planning, provider-ready search and test or fallback experiences where live commercial inventory or transactions are not yet available. We do not present planning tools as a bank, payment service or guaranteed booking marketplace.

Our principles

  • Useful context: suggestions should reflect the trip, not just a generic destination.
  • Clarity: users should know what is live, test, backend or fallback.
  • Control: important additions and shared access should require the traveller's choice.
  • Respect: private trip information should be handled with care.
