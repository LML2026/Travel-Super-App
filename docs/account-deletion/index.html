Delete your ITAREVO Finance account

If you would like to delete your ITAREVO Finance account and the personal data associated with it, you can request account deletion by contacting ITAREVO support.

How to request deletion

Send an email from the email address linked to your ITAREVO Finance account to: support@itarevo.com

Please use the subject line: ITAREVO Finance account deletion request

What will be deleted

Once your request is verified, we will delete your ITAREVO Finance account and personal data associated with the account, except where information must be retained for legal, regulatory, fraud-prevention, security, accounting, or other legitimate compliance purposes.

Retention

Data that must be retained for legal or regulatory reasons may be kept for the period required by applicable law and will then be securely deleted or anonymised.

Need help?

If you have questions about account deletion or privacy, contact support@itarevo.com.
