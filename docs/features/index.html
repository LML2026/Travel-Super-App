ITAREVO Travel

A complete toolkit for a more capable journey.

One trip-aware place for planning before you leave, navigating while you travel and keeping the details together afterwards.

01

Trips and itinerary

Create a trip and bring flights, accommodation, transport, activities and restaurant plans into a chronological view.

02

AI Travel Companion

Generate local plans using dates, pace, interests, budget, weather and existing trip context. Review suggestions before adding them.

03

Discovery

Search provider-ready flights, hotels, transport planning, activities, restaurants and useful nearby places with clear source labels.

04

Maps and routes

Move from an itinerary location or nearby place into route and navigation handoffs through the existing Google Maps integration.

05

Live Trip

See the next event, today's timeline, weather, nearby essentials, documents, translator, expenses and readiness when the trip is underway.

06

Spoken translator

Translate text, use phrasebook and conversation tools, speak input and play translations when device capabilities support them.

07

Weather and nearby

Use live Open-Meteo weather where available, then find restaurants, cafés, attractions and essential services near your destination.

08

Readiness and documents

Track preparation tasks, reminders, confirmations and travel references in the context of the trip.

09

Budget and saved items

Record expenses, understand remaining spend and save useful flights, hotels and places for later planning.

Clear product boundaries

Planning first. Honest about what is live.

ITAREVO can connect to live and test provider services, but not every result is a live commercial booking. The experience identifies test, backend, demo and fallback data so you can plan with the right expectations.
